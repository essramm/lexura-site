Scheduled maintenance

  • 30k, 60k and 90k serviceFilters, fluids, plugs and a full inspection, priced for your car rather than sold as a package.
  • Oil and fluid changesEngine oil, transmission, coolant, brake and power steering at the intervals your model calls for.
  • Timing belts and water pumpsBelt, tensioner, idlers, pump and seals done together so the front of the engine opens once.

Repair and diagnosis

  • BrakesPads and rotors measured before anything is quoted.
  • Check engine and drivabilityCodes narrow the search. We trace the fault to the failing part before replacing it.
  • Cooling and overheatingRadiators, thermostats, hoses and pumps, diagnosed properly.
  • Suspension and steeringStruts, bushings and axles on higher mileage RX, Camry, Accord and TL platforms.

Before you buy, and after 100k

  • Hybrid serviceRoutine maintenance, inverter coolant and brake work on Prius, Camry and RX hybrids.
